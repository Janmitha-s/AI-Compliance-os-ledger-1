SECTION 137(1) Copy of financial statement to be filed with Registrar
Illustrative paraphrase, not the verbatim Act text — replace with the
authoritative text from India Code before relying on this for real advice.
A company must file its adopted financial statements, along with all
required attachments, with the Registrar within the statutory window
following its Annual General Meeting. Where the AGM was not held, the
filing window is instead counted from the date the AGM ought to have
been held under this Act, together with a statement of the reasons for
not holding it.

SECTION 137(3) Consequences of failure to file
Illustrative paraphrase. If a company fails to file as required under
this section, the company becomes liable to an additional fee for the
period of default, and both the company and every officer in default
become liable to further penalty as prescribed.

SECTION 403 Fee for filings, etc.
Illustrative paraphrase. Where a document, fact or information required
to be submitted is filed after the period provided under the specific
section, it may still be filed on payment of such additional fee as may
be prescribed, and different fees may be prescribed for different
classes of companies or different forms.

SECTION 164(2) Disqualification of directors
Illustrative paraphrase. A person who is or has been a director of a
company that has failed to file financial statements or annual returns
for a continuous period of three financial years shall not be eligible
for re-appointment as a director of that company or appointment in any
other company for a period of five years from the date of default.
